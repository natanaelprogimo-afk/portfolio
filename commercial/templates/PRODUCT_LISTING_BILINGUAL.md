## Listing media review

- No fabricated dashboard, client logo, review, team photo or certification badge.
- Real product screenshots with personally identifiable information redacted.
- Alt text and caption identify version/context; label mockups as mockups.
- Disclose AI uncertainty, model/provider dependence and human approval.
- Checkout shows actual full price and renewal terms; no fake countdowns or scarcity.
- Business/legal names and links match invoices and documentation.
- Test responsive layouts, keyboard navigation, translation and payment cancellation.

# Customer onboarding, delivery, renewals and offboarding template

**No live operation or guaranteed service is represented by this template.**

## Onboarding
1. Confirm signed scope, real seller, account eligibility, legal requirements and customer permissions.
2. Reconcile processor payment receipt and verify refund/chargeback status; do not mark money received until settlement.
3. Provision tenant with scoped credentials, least privilege, budget cap and explicit user authorization.
4. Record enabled integrations and tool approval rules; show preview of sensitive actions.
5. Test primary workflow with synthetic fixture and confirm customer acceptance in writing.
6. Send truthful support/privacy/cancel/export links and version number.

## Service lifecycle
- Monitor uptime and API/provider failures without false SLA assurances.
- Track changes to model/provider costs and customer-consented usage caps.
- Rate-limit activity; audit side effects and idempotency on retries.
- Notify customer of material changes in accepted contractual conditions.
- Maintain rollback and backup restoration records where applicable.
- For autonomous agents, enforce immediate stop and approval on escalating permissions.

## Renewal/cancellation
- Explain renewal timing, invoices, tax and additional usage charges before charging.
- Make cancellation instructions accessible and honor contractual terms.
- Revoke agent/API access and schedule data export/deletion after termination.
- Reconcile final charges/refunds/payouts separately from subscription flag.
- Provide clear support path for contested transactions and security complaints.
